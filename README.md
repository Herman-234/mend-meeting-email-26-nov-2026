Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9): one line, at most 200 characters, a joke about a meeting that should have been an email.

> Our 47-minute standup could have been a 9-word email: 'Deploy delayed. Blame the database. Sorry, Dave.'

`main.py` deterministically writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (104 chars, UTF-8, no trailing newline). Three candidate lines were drafted in-session; the sharpest was picked because the email IS the joke, a named victim (Dave) takes the blame, and two hard numbers (47 minutes, 9 words) make the waste concrete.
